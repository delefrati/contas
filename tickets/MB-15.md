The playlist dialog (MB-17) lists the songs without a Spotify link, each with a field to paste
one. Pasting means leaving the app to find the track. Add a **Buscar** button next to each of
those songs that searches Spotify for it, shows a few tracks, and lets a member pick the version
the band plays. One song at a time, when someone asks: no sweep over the whole repertoire.
Nothing is saved until a track is picked, because the first result is often not the version the
band plays.

- **Check first:** that `GET /search` with `type=track` still works for a Development Mode app
  after Spotify's 2026 changes, and its current `limit`. If it doesn't, stop and say so.
- **The search:** a new band endpoint searches with the song's title and, when there is one, its
  artist, using the platform's connection (`SpotifyService.getAccessToken()`, MB-16), and answers
  with the first 5 tracks: name, artists, album, year, length, cover and link. Nothing about the
  account reaches the page.
- **Picking:** the tracks show under the song, with their cover, and each opens in Spotify to
  listen before choosing. **Usar esta** saves the link through the existing song update (MB-13),
  and the song leaves the list of songs without a link, as when a link is pasted.
- **Nothing found:** the song says "nada encontrado"; the paste field is still there.
- **A song without an artist** is searched by its title alone.
- **Spotify unavailable, or asking to wait:** the same messages as the playlist (MB-17),
  `SPOTIFY_UNAVAILABLE` and `SPOTIFY_RATE_LIMITED`, under the song.
- **Offline:** the button is off, like the rest of the dialog.
- **Band isolation:** the endpoint takes a song ID and reads the song only within the request's
  band, so a member can't search, or learn the title of, another band's song; the pick is saved
  through the song update, which refuses a song of another band.
- **The docs:** `evento.md` and `musicas.md` mention **Buscar** in the playlist dialog, and the
  `spotify-playlist` screenshot is taken again.

**Done when:** backend tests with a mocked Spotify show the query with and without an artist, the
tracks mapped, nothing found, a song of another band refused and a 429 reported; a spec shows
searching, picking (which saves the link) and nothing found; `/verify` shows a song found and
picked in the dialog against Spotify; the docs above are updated.
