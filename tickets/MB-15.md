Pasting a link song by song is slow for a band with a hundred songs. Let the app search Spotify
for the songs that have no link yet, all of the band's or only the ones in an event's setlist,
and let a member pick the right version of each. Nothing is saved until the member picks it,
because the first result is often not the version the band plays.

- **Where it starts:** **Buscar no Spotify** in Músicas, for every song without a link, and
  **Buscar links do repertório** on an event's page, for the songs in its setlist without one.
- **The search:** the backend searches Spotify with the title and the artist, using the app's
  own key (Client Credentials, as MB-14 settles), never a key in the browser, and answers with
  the first few tracks: name, artists, album, year, length and cover.
- **Picking:** one song at a time, the member picks one of the tracks, listens to it in Spotify
  first if they want, or skips the song. A pick saves the link as in MB-13.
- **Nothing found,** or a song without an artist: the song shows "nada encontrado" and can be
  skipped; a member can still paste a link by hand.
- **Songs that already have a link** are left out, so the search never changes a version the
  band chose.
- **Spotify's limits:** the songs are searched a few at a time; a 429 waits for `Retry-After`
  and a Spotify outage shows an error in Portuguese without losing the picks already saved.
- **Not configured:** without the Spotify keys in the server's `.env`, the buttons don't show.
- **Band isolation:** the song list and the setlist come from the existing queries filtered by
  the request's band, and a pick is saved through the song update, which refuses a song of
  another band.
- **The docs:** `musicas.md` and `evento.md` explain the search, with a screenshot of it;
  `DEPLOY-OCI.md` and `backend-nest/.env.example` list the Spotify keys.

**Done when:** a backend test shows the search with a mocked Spotify, songs with a link left
out and a 429 retried; specs show picking and skipping; `/verify` shows a setlist's songs found
and one picked, its link surviving a reload; the docs above are updated.
