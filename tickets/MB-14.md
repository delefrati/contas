Before building on the Spotify Web API, settle the questions that decide how MB-16 and MB-17 are
built. Write the answers down; build nothing.

- **The app's quota:** a new Spotify app runs in Development Mode, open only to the Spotify
  accounts added to it by hand, and Spotify has tightened who gets extended quota. Check what
  the limits are today and whether minhabanda.com can live with them (every band's member who
  connects an account would have to be added), or what the alternative is.
- **Whose account owns the playlist:** each member connects their own Spotify, or the band keeps
  one shared account. Which one the playlist belongs to, and who can update it later.
- **The redirect on band subdomains:** Spotify accepts only exact redirect URIs, and each band
  has its own subdomain. Check that one callback on the central host, handing back to the band
  the way the Google sign-in does, works, and what the `state` must carry.
- **Tokens:** where the refresh token is kept and how it is encrypted at rest, with which key.
- **The scopes** the playlist needs (`playlist-modify-private`, `playlist-modify-public`), and
  nothing more.

**Done when:** a short write-up in `DEPLOY-OCI.md` (the Spotify app, its keys and redirect URI)
and in MB-16 and MB-17 records the answers, and those tickets are changed where an answer
changes them.
