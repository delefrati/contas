id: MB-11
title: "Stop the browser from translating the app"
status: done
sprint: 1
rank: 2

epic: frontend
---

Chrome, Edge and Safari offer to translate the page, and some members have them set to do it
automatically. `frontend/index.html` declares `<html lang="en">` whatever language the app is
showing, so a browser in Portuguese sees "an English page" and translates Portuguese into
Portuguese. The translation rewrites song titles, lyrics, setlists and people's names, and
Google Translate wraps text in `<font>` tags that Vue doesn't know about, which can break the
page when Vue next updates it (a blank screen, `removeChild` errors in the console). The app has
its own language selector, so the browser shouldn't translate on top of it.

- **Opting out:** `<html translate="no">` and `<meta name="google" content="notranslate">` in
  `index.html`, so the browser neither offers nor applies a translation, on every screen.
- **The page's language:** `<html lang>` starts as `pt-BR`, matching `manifest.webmanifest`,
  and follows the app's locale (`pt-BR`, `en`, `es`) on load and when it's changed in
  `LanguageSelector`.
- **The installed app:** the PWA on a phone doesn't offer a translation either.
- **The app's own languages:** unchanged. The PT/EN/ES selector, the choice saved in
  `localStorage` and the browser-language guess on a first visit work as before.
- **The manual (MB-10):** blocked like every other screen; in EN and ES it keeps its one-line
  note that it's in Portuguese only.

**Done when:** a spec shows `document.documentElement.lang` following the locale at start and
after switching, and `index.html` has `translate="no"` and the `notranslate` meta; checked in
Chrome on the desktop and on Android, with the browser in Portuguese and in English, that no
translation is offered on the dashboard, an event's page and a song's lyrics; `MULTILINGUAL.md`
says the app opts out of browser translation and why.
