The song list (`/musicas`) shows each song's title with nothing before it, so a band can't say
"the 12th" or see at a glance how many songs it has. Put a number before each title: `1.`, `2.`,
`3.` and so on, in the order the list already shows.

- **The number:** the song's place in the whole repertoire, in the list's current order, written
  as `1.` before the title, in the same cell, in a muted style so the title still reads first.
- **Filtering:** a song keeps its number when the filter hides others, so a search that finds
  only the 12th song shows `12.`, not `1.`.
- **Mobile:** the number stays on the title's line and doesn't push the actions out of place.
- **Offline copy:** the list shown from the saved copy is numbered the same way.
- **The docs:** `musicas.md` says the songs are numbered, and the `musicas` screenshot is taken
  again.

**Done when:** a spec in `Songs.spec.js` shows the numbers in order and a filtered song keeping
its number; `/verify` shows the numbered list on desktop and mobile; `musicas.md` and its
screenshot are updated.
