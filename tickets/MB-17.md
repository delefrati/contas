To rehearse, the band wants the songs in the versions it plays in one Spotify playlist. Make
one from all of the band's songs, or from an event's setlist in its order, in minhabanda.com's
Spotify account (MB-16), and keep it up to date when it is made again. The band gets a link that
opens it.

- **Where it starts:** **Playlist no Spotify** in Músicas, for all songs, and on an event's page,
  for its setlist. Any active member can use it, as with everything else in the band.
- **The order:** the setlist's order for an event, with the pauses left out; the list's order
  for all songs.
- **Songs without a link** are left out, and the app says which before making the playlist, with
  a way to open each song and paste its link (MB-13).
- **Public, with a careful name:** the playlist is public, since a private one can't be opened
  by others (MB-14), so anyone with the link can open it and it shows on the account's profile.
  Its name has only the band and the date, such as "Maverick · 12/12/2026", or the band's name and
  "Repertório" for all songs; never the event's title or place.
- **Making it again** replaces the tracks of the same playlist instead of making a new one: the
  playlist's ID is kept on the event, and on the band for all songs, with a Prisma migration. A
  playlist Spotify no longer finds (404) is made again.
- **The calls:** `POST /me/playlists` to create one, `PUT /playlists/{id}/items` with the first
  100 tracks and `POST /playlists/{id}/items` for each further 100.
- **No working connection:** the button doesn't show (not set up) or says Spotify is
  unavailable for now (connection broken), and a super admin is the one to fix it.
- **Spotify's limits:** a `429`, `QUOTA_EXCEEDED` or not, says to try again later; other errors
  show a message in Portuguese.
- **Offline:** the button is off without internet.
- **Band isolation:** the songs and the setlist come from the existing queries filtered by the
  request's band, and the stored playlist IDs are read and written only for that band and its
  events. The platform's tokens never reach the page.
- **The logs:** making a playlist is written to the band's Registros.
- **The docs:** `musicas.md` and `evento.md` explain the playlist and that it is public, with a
  screenshot.

**Done when:** backend tests with a mocked Spotify show the setlist's order kept, pauses and
songs without a link left out, the name without the event's title, a second run replacing the
tracks, 150 songs sent as 100 and 50, a missing playlist made again and a 429 reported; specs
show the warning about songs without a link and the button without a connection; `/verify`
shows an event's playlist made and opened in Spotify; the docs above are updated.
