To rehearse, the band wants the songs in the versions it plays in one Spotify playlist. Make
one from all of the band's songs, or from an event's setlist in its order, in the connected
account, and keep it up to date when it is made again.

- **Where it starts:** **Playlist no Spotify** in Músicas, for all songs, and on an event's page,
  for its setlist.
- **The order:** the setlist's order for an event, with the pauses and other items that are not
  songs left out; the list's order for all songs.
- **Songs without a link** are left out, and the app says which before making the playlist,
  with a way to open each song and paste its link (MB-13).
- **The name:** the band's name and the event's title and date, such as "Maverick - Show no
  Bar - 12/12/2026", or the band's name and "Repertório" for all songs; private by default.
- **Making it again** replaces the tracks of the same playlist instead of making a new one: the
  playlist's ID is kept on the event, and on the band for all songs, with a Prisma migration. A
  playlist deleted in Spotify is made again.
- **More than 100 songs:** the tracks are added in batches of 100, Spotify's limit.
- **No account connected:** the button leads to Configurações (MB-16).
- **The result:** a link that opens the playlist in Spotify; a Spotify error shows a message in
  Portuguese.
- **Offline:** the button is off without internet.
- **Band isolation:** the songs and the setlist come from the existing queries filtered by the
  request's band, and the playlist is made with the connection of that band only.
- **The logs:** making a playlist is written to the Registros.
- **The docs:** `musicas.md` and `evento.md` explain the playlist, with a screenshot.

**Done when:** backend tests with a mocked Spotify show the setlist's order kept, pauses and
songs without a link left out, a second run replacing the tracks, 150 songs sent in two
batches and a deleted playlist made again; specs show the warning about songs without a link;
`/verify` shows an event's playlist made and opened in Spotify; the docs above are updated.
