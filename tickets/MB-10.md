There's no guide for the people who use minhabanda.com: a new member learns the app by asking
someone, and the README and `DEPLOY-OCI.md` are written for whoever runs the server. Write a
manual for members and band admins, in Portuguese, with screenshots, and show it in the app
at `/ajuda`, so help is a tap away from any screen and the same link can be sent to a new
member.

The manual is markdown, one file per part of the app, in `frontend/src/manual/`, bundled at
build time and rendered by a `Manual` page. It's the only copy: the README links to those files
instead of keeping another.

What the manual covers:

- **Getting in:** signing in with Google, the central login and the band picker, and what to
  do when your account isn't in a band yet.
- **Each screen:** the dashboard, the agenda and an event's page (setlist, the phone view, how
  far through the setlist it is), the repertoire, the songs and their lyrics, the members,
  the finances and the logs: what it's for and the common tasks on it, step by step.
- **Who can do what:** what a member, a band admin and a super admin can each see and change,
  including that a member is disabled, never deleted.
- **On the phone:** installing the app, what works offline (agenda, setlists, lyrics), and
  subscribing to the band's agenda from your own calendar.
- **Settings:** changing the language (PT, EN, ES).
- **Super admins:** creating a band and managing super admins under `/admin`, in a page of its
  own, shown only to super admins.
- **Screenshots** from a local stack with made-up data (see the `/verify` skill), never from
  production, so no real band's data ends up in the repo.

What the app does with it:

- **The `/ajuda` route:** named `Manual`, with a `titleKey`, an index of the parts and a page
  for each, such as `/ajuda/agenda`; an unknown part goes to the index.
- **Before signing in:** the route has `requiresAuth: false` and the login page links to it,
  so someone who can't get in can still read "Getting in".
- **From every screen:** a "Help" link in `AppHeader`, and in `AdminLayout` for super admins.
  Where a screen has a part of its own, the link opens that part.
- **Offline:** the route is marked `offline: true` like the agenda, and the text and
  screenshots are cached with the app, so it opens with no connection.
- **Other languages:** the manual is in Portuguese only for now. In EN and ES the page shows
  it with a one-line note saying so, translated in `pt.json`, `en.json` and `es.json`.
- **Band isolation:** the manual is static and the same for every band; the page reads no band
  data and calls no API.

**Done when:** a spec shows `/ajuda` and a part's page render signed out and signed in, and an
unknown part falls back to the index; `/verify` shows the Help link on each screen opening its
part, the login page link, and the manual opening offline on an installed phone view; each
part was checked against the running app; the README links the manual.
